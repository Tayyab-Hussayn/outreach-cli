Rush hours mein WhatsApp par jo orders late reply ki wajah se miss ho jate hain, wo aksar Foodpanda par chale jate hain, aur wahan har order par commission kat-ti hai.

Is ke liye maine ek WhatsApp AI Assistant banaya hai:

1. Har customer ko instant reply, menu aur dish pictures ke saath
2. Order khud leta hai, total calculate karta hai, aur customer se confirm karwata hai
3. Confirmed order ki detail seedha kitchen ke WhatsApp par
4. Confused customer ko restaurant ka call number de deta hai, taake wo direct baat kar sake

Direct WhatsApp orders par koi commission nahi.
